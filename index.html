    Welcome to your portfolio dashboard

    Get started in under a minute. Everything you enter stays in this browser.

    1. Add your holdings. Enter each purchase with Add trade, or bring them all in from a spreadsheet with Import CSV.
    2. Pick the account. Tag trades as TFSA, RRSP, FHSA or non-registered to see each one separately and get a capital-gains report for taxable accounts.
    3. Explore. See dividends, risk, rebalancing, news and why the market moved.
